2

Configure Settings

⚙️ Configuration Options

cursedErrors.enable

boolean

Enable or disable the extension

Default: true

cursedErrors.intensity

low | medium | high

Control the intensity of effects

Default: medium

cursedErrors.theme

darkSmoke | purpleCurse | cracks

Choose your visual theme

Default: darkSmoke

cursedErrors.triggerLevel

errorOnly | errorAndWarning | all

Set when effects should trigger

Default: errorOnly

cursedErrors.scope

activeEditor | allEditors

Apply effects to active or all editors

Default: activeEditor

cursedErrors.cooldownMs

number

Cooldown time between effects (ms)

Default: 300
